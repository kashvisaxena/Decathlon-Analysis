Decathlon — Management Control, Strategy & KPI Analysis

Project Overview

This project analyses Decathlon from a management control and strategic performance perspective.

The project combines external environment analysis, competitive analysis, business model evaluation and performance measurement.

The main objective was to connect Decathlon’s strategic priorities with measurable KPIs through a Strategy Map and Balanced Scorecard.

Analysis Scope

The project focused on:

- PESTEL analysis
- Porter’s Five Forces
- Competitor comparison with Nike and Adidas
- Business model and supply-chain analysis
- Sales channels and omnichannel strategy
- Sustainability and innovation
- Strategy Map development
- Balanced Scorecard
- KPI, target and initiative development

Key Insights

External Environment

The PESTEL analysis identified important factors affecting Decathlon, including economic conditions, digitalisation, sustainability expectations, regulation and changing consumer behaviour.

Competitive Position

Porter’s Five Forces showed strong competitive rivalry and a high threat of substitutes. Decathlon’s scale, vertically integrated model and supply-chain control were identified as important competitive strengths.

Strategic Positioning

The analysis highlighted accessibility, cost-effectiveness, vertical integration, innovation and sustainability as important parts of Decathlon’s positioning.

Performance Management

The Strategy Map connected objectives across four perspectives:

- Financial
- Customer
- Internal Processes
- Learning & Growth

The Balanced Scorecard translated these strategic objectives into measurable KPIs, targets and initiatives.

Examples included B2B revenue growth, supply-chain cost reduction, customer satisfaction, repeat purchase, omnichannel performance, inventory tracking, digital skills and sustainability initiatives.

Overall Conclusion

The project demonstrated how strategic analysis can be converted into a structured performance management system.

Decathlon’s strategy combines affordability, operational control, innovation, digitalisation and sustainability. The Strategy Map and Balanced Scorecard connected these priorities with measurable performance indicators and management actions.

Tools & Skills

Tools:
Microsoft PowerPoint, strategic frameworks, KPI design and management control methods

Skills demonstrated:
Management control · Strategic analysis · KPI development · Balanced Scorecard · Strategy Map · PESTEL · Porter’s Five Forces · Competitor analysis · Business reporting · Teamwork
